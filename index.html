FAQ

Before you pull up

What are Gummy’s Home Cooking’s hours?

Friday and Saturday from 12 PM to 6 PM, and Sunday from 12 PM to 5 PM. Gummy’s is closed Monday through Thursday. Hours occasionally change (for example, an early close on a Sunday), so check Instagram for day-of updates.

Can I call ahead and place an order?

Yes. Call (804) 447-5907 to place your order ahead of time, then pick it up at 7503 Midlothian Tpke.

Is Gummy’s dine-in or takeout?

Both. You can sit down and eat in, pull up to the bar, or take your plates to go.

Do the entrees come with sides?

Yes. Every entree comes with 2 sides, like May-Belle Yams, Girlie Greens, Swamp Cabbage, Perlene Potato Salad or Shellie’s Mac & Cheese.

How much does a plate cost?

On the printed menu, entrees with 2 sides run $18 to $25, sandwiches with fries run $13.99 to $15.99, and sides run $3.50 to $6.50. Kids meals with fries and a drink start at $5.99.

Where is Gummy’s located?

7503 Midlothian Tpke, Richmond, VA 23225, on Midlothian Turnpike in Southside Richmond. It sits between the Latino Market and Goodyear.

When can I get Buddy’s Beef Ribs?

Buddy’s Beef Ribs are a Sunday-only entree. Call ahead on Sunday to make sure they’re still available.

Is Gummy’s Home Cooking Black-owned?

Yes. Gummy’s is a Black-owned soul food restaurant in Richmond, VA.
