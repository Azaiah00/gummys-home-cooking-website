FAQ

Visiting questions

Do I need a reservation?

No reservation system is listed. Come by during open hours to dine in or order takeout, or call (804) 447-5907 ahead with your order.

Is there parking?

Gummy’s is on Midlothian Turnpike between the Latino Market and Goodyear. For the best place to park, call (804) 447-5907 before you head over.

Does Gummy’s ever close early?

Occasionally. For example, Gummy’s has posted Sunday early closings at 4 PM. Day-of changes are posted on Instagram, so check there before you go.

What days is Gummy’s open?

Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Friday and Saturday 12 PM to 6 PM, Sunday 12 PM to 5 PM. Closed Monday through Thursday.
