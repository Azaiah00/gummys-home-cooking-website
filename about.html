Our story

A family kitchen on Midlothian Turnpike.

Black-owned, Southside, and serving soul food with a family name on every entree.

Gummy’s Home Cooking is a Black-owned soul food restaurant at 7503 Midlothian Tpke in Southside Richmond, between the Latino Market and Goodyear. The doors open on the weekend: Friday and Saturday from noon to 6, and Sunday from noon to 5.

The menu reads like a family reunion. The fried fish is Papa’s. The fried shrimp is Elma’s. There are Susie’s Chicken Wings, Johnnie’s Smothered Porkchops, Charlie’s Baked Turkey Wings, Bertha’s Baked Chicken and Wilbur’s Pig Feet, and on Sundays, Buddy’s Beef Ribs. Every entree carries the name of someone in the family.

Each one comes with 2 sides, from May-Belle Yams to Girlie Greens. Specials rotate through the week: shrimp po’ boys, cheese steak egg rolls, crab dip and chips, homemade rolls by the tray, peach cobbler and cakes in the case.

Come in and eat, pull up a seat at the bar, or call ahead and take it home.
